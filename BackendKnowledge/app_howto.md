# How to use AI HELP
Click the AI HELP button at the top right.
A text box appears at the bottom center.
Type a question.
Press SEND.
Lisa answers with short steps for how-to questions.
If the player asks how their work looks, Lisa uses the live sculpture data (shape, colors, stamps, bands, selfie, vertex sculpt) and gives specific praise through OpenAI.
AI HELP does not send a question by itself.

# Where to go in MORPHIC
Pick a country on the main map.
Taiwan opens the lantern.
India opens the teapot.
Indonesia opens the keris.
Sculpt with hand gestures first.
Then the COLOR PALETTE opens for color, stamps, bands, selfie, and vertex sculpt.

# How to skip the gesture tutorial
On a gesture practice card, press SKIP if you already know the moves.
That jumps straight into sculpting.

# How to lock a shape
After you change the sculpture with a gesture, open your palm.
Open palm locks the shape so other gestures do not undo it.

# How to ask where a button is
Type the thing you want, like change tassel color, add a stamp, or take a selfie.
Lisa names the exact on-screen buttons.
Use currentTool: lantern, teapot, or keris.
